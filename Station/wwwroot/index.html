No operator

Operator

Entering a class attests what you hold. This desk does not look you up in the FCC database. A grid is your Maidenhead square. Look up turns that square into coordinates, or fills it from the published call sign when the box is empty. That lookup is not a license check. Transmit stays locked until you check the box and click Transmit on a message.

Antenna

A vertical antenna is a quarter-wave. A horizontal wire is a half-wave. Guess listens to the noise floor and does not transmit. If the receiver already has the radio open, the guess uses that radio.

Set the length and orientation. Matching bands are listed under it.

    Draft

    You or an agent can write the text. Holding a draft does not transmit. After you authorize, CW and digimodes (FT8, JS8, Olivia, JTTY, Packet) can be played as local audio. The message has to include your call sign.

    Repeater phone

    FM phone on a repeater or simplex. Save machines you use often, then pick one. Output is the published listen frequency. Input is what you transmit, and Part 97 has to allow that frequency for your license before you can hold a draft. Encode opens the machine. Decode is optional receive squelch. Holding a draft does not transmit.

    Set the output and shift. Standard offsets fill in for the band.
