Natural Earth vector data (public domain)
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector

Bundled offline basemap layers (no tile servers):
- land.geojson — ne_110m_land
- lakes.geojson — ne_110m_lakes
- rivers.geojson — ne_110m_rivers_lake_centerlines
- countries.geojson — ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land (international boundaries)
- states.geojson — ne_110m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines
- country-labels.geojson / state-labels.geojson — label points derived from Natural Earth admin polygons
- peaks.geojson — major mountains from ne_50m_geography_regions_elevation_points (elevation ASL)
