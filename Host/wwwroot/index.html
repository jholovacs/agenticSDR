What these words mean

This page only receives. A longer lesson, with the same definitions, is in the project file GUIDE.md.

SDR
Software-defined radio. The USB stick captures a slice of radio spectrum, and this program decides what the signal is.
Frequency
How fast the radio wave repeats, in MHz (millions of cycles per second). 146.520 MHz is the 2 m calling spot.
Band
A named range of frequencies. “2 m” means wavelengths around 2 meters, about 144–148 MHz. “40 m” is near 7 MHz.
HF
Below 24 MHz on this RTL-SDR the tuner steps aside and the chip samples the antenna more directly. You need an HF antenna. A short 2 m whip hears little there.
Vertical
The wire stands up. Treated as a quarter-wave: about one quarter of the wavelength it hears best.
Horizontal
The wire runs sideways. Treated as a half-wave. Enter the compass direction one end points.
° true
Degrees clockwise from true north. 0 is north, 90 is east. A horizontal wire hears best at right angles to itself.
Prompt
The sentence you type for the agent, such as “Scan 2 m and tell me what you hear.” The agent may scan, listen, or change the antenna. Stop cancels that work.
Instruct model
A language model that follows written directions. This page talks to it with the OpenAI chat protocol, at an address ending in /v1, so a local server can stand in for OpenAI.
Endpoint
That address. The API key, if the server wants one, is stored on this computer and is not shown again. It is sent only to the endpoint you saved.
Tool
An action the model can ask for, such as listing bands or scanning. The model does not tune the radio itself. This program does the work and gives the result back.
Guess
Listens at several spots and treats the loudest noise floor as the antenna’s natural frequency. A vertical length is a quarter of that wave. A horizontal wire would be half of it. Strength does not show which way the wire points.
Best
A band near the antenna’s natural frequency, including odd multiples of that frequency.
Scan
Hop across the band, measure the spectrum, and list peaks that stand out from the noise.
Mode
How the voice or data was put onto the wave. Auto guesses. The choices are defined below.
AM
The loudness of the audio changes the strength of the wave. This is AM broadcast and some amateur signals.
NBFM
Narrow FM. The voice nudges the frequency a little. Amateur repeaters and NOAA weather use this.
WBFM
Wide FM. The same idea, but the station is about 200 kHz wide. This is ordinary FM broadcast, 88–108 MHz.
USB / LSB
Single-sideband voice. Amateur HF uses LSB on the lower bands and USB on the higher ones.
CW
Morse code. An on-off carrier. Listen plays a tone and tries to print the letters.
CB
Citizens Band, 40 channels near 27 MHz. Most traffic is AM or narrow FM. Channel 9 is the emergency channel and channel 19 is the highway channel. Receive only.
GMRS
General Mobile Radio Service, narrow FM near 462 and 467 MHz. Family radios and repeaters use these channels. Receive only.
DMR
A digital voice mode on parts of 2 m and 70 cm. The receiver recognizes the framing. The words use a proprietary codec, so they are not decoded. Receive only.
Speech
Spoken audio from AM, FM, or sideband, written out as text. Those analog voice modes have pops removed and the sound limited to the voice range before a small speech model, downloaded once into the models folder, reads it. Morse and digital signals are not filtered and are not transcribed. The text is a guess, the same way a person might mis-hear a noisy channel.
FT8
A short digital message sent in 15-second turns. The radio waits for the next turn, then prints any messages it can read.
Local spur
A steady whistle from the dongle or the computer, often a harmonic of its clock. If it has no voice, Morse, or FT8, the scan skips it.
SNR
How many decibels the signal sits above the noise. Bigger is cleaner.
Conf
How sure the mode guess is. Listening is the check.
Source
A label from the band plan, such as a calling frequency, “FM broadcast,” or a NOAA channel. It is not a station’s name.
NOAA
US weather radio near 162.55 MHz. The NOAA button listens to one channel so you can tell the radio is working.
